# Photo Ownership Phases 1-10

1. **Owner photo priority** — owner uploads and owner-selected cover photos are authoritative; Google cannot overwrite the hero.
2. **Claim photo setup** — newly approved claims route into the photo setup experience.
3. **Profile strength** — owner-photo completeness contributes to a transparent profile completion score.
4. **Photo manager** — upload, reorder, remove, and set a cover photo from the location dashboard.
5. **Five-photo Google gap fill** — indexed Google photo slots fill only missing public gallery positions.
6. **Gentle reminders** — day-3/day-7 account reminders stop once three owner photos are reached.
7. **Analytics** — owner upload and reminder funnel events plus authoritative reminder counters.
8. **Admin/CRM photo health** — low-pressure CRM follow-up tasks and an admin photo-health API.
9. **Search quality signal** — owner photography adds at most +3 to the existing search boost.
10. **Google cost/compliance hardening** — no-store media proxy, current attribution, monthly request cap, and bypass of known legacy Google snapshots.
