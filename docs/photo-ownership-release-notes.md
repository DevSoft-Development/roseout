# Photo Ownership Release Notes

TheOutHaven location profiles now prefer business-owned imagery and can fill empty gallery positions with current Google Places photos. Claimed businesses are guided toward three photos as the recommended minimum and five photos as a complete gallery.

The rollout also adds Google attribution, a monthly Google photo media budget cap, owner-photo overwrite protection, profile completion tracking, gentle reminders, CRM/admin photo-health signals, and a small search-quality tie-breaker for complete owner-controlled profiles.
